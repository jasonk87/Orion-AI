Why can't I connect to 5GHz WiFi?

Distance/walls — 5GHz has shorter range than 2.4GHz. Move closer to your router and try again.
Device doesn't support 5GHz — check specs for "802.11ac", "Wi-Fi 5", or "Wi-Fi 6". Older/budget devices are often 2.4GHz only.
Outdated driver (Windows) — Device Manager → Network Adapters → right-click adapter → Update driver.
5GHz disabled on router — log into your router at 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1 and check the 5GHz band is on.
Wrong password — if your router has separate names per band (e.g. "MyWifi_5G"), it may have a different password.
Does the 5GHz network appear in your list but fail to connect, or does it not show up at all? That'll help narrow it down.
