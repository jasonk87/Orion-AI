# Why Can't I Connect to 5GHz WiFi?

Common causes and fixes:

**Distance/walls** — 5GHz has shorter range than 2.4GHz. Try moving closer to the router.

**Device doesn't support 5GHz** — look for "802.11ac", "Wi-Fi 5", or "Wi-Fi 6" in your specs. Older/budget devices are often 2.4GHz only.

**Outdated drivers (Windows)** — Device Manager → Network Adapters → right-click your adapter → Update driver.

**Router has 5GHz disabled** — log into your router at 192.168.1.1 or 192.168.0.1 and check the 5GHz band is on.

**Wrong password** — if it's a separate network (e.g. "MyWifi_5G"), make sure you're using the right password for that band.

Does the 5GHz network show up in your WiFi list but fail to connect, or does it not appear at all? That'll help narrow it down further.
